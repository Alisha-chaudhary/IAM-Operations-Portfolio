## Evidence type definitions

- **Actual lab evidence:** Generated by executing against the local IdentityGuard API and database. Sanitised API responses and reports (no secrets).
- **Simulated stakeholder evidence:** Business reviewer judgments simulated per-department and recorded in decision justifications. Explicitly labelled as simulated.
- **Policy analysis:** Analysis of governance documents (Access Control Policy, RBAC Matrix, Entitlement Catalogue, SoD Register).
